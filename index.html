A shop built around one worktable.

Belle’s started in 2014 as a folding table at the Saturday farmers market. Twelve years later, it’s a proper studio on Maple Street — but every order still passes through the same hands before it goes out the door.

Flowers are ordered close to when they’ll be used, cut and conditioned the same morning, and hand-tied by whoever is at the table that day.

What stays the same every order

Seasonal & local first

Stems come from nearby growers whenever the season allows.

Same morning, one pair of hands

One florist takes an order from stem selection through to the final tie.

Rooted in the neighborhood

A portion of every wedding booking supports Willow Creek’s community garden.
